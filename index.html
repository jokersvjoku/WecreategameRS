01 / THE EVIDENCE

What students report.

Seven recurring concerns gathered from public reviews, explainers and complaint forums. Each report is paired with a practical verification step.

7 / 7 ISSUES
Status

NIAT is not a college and cannot give you a degree

NIAT describes itself as an upskilling programme run by NxtWave. The B.Tech degree comes from a partner university, and the training is delivered inside that university's campus. Careers360 and NIAT's own explainer pages say this plainly. The name "Institute" and the campus-style marketing can make it sound like one institution, and one review site notes that official marketing talks more about NxtWave than about any single campus.

Careers360 also raises three questions for buyers: who you are paying, whose campus you are studying on, and who is answerable if something goes wrong.

  • Careers360
  • NIAT Insider
  • New Age Colleges
Before you pay

Ask for the university's own admission letter and fee receipt. Find out how much of your fee goes to the university and how much to NxtWave, and get that split in writing.

Regulation

A reported UGC notice about collaboration programmes

Careers360 reports that on 27 March 2026 the University Grants Commission issued a notice against NIAT concerning degree programmes offered in collaboration with 25 recognised universities. As Careers360 describes it, the notice says universities may not run programmes through franchising or collaboration with private entities in distance or online mode without the Commission's approval.

I could not locate the notice itself, so treat this as a secondhand report. The wording as reported covers distance and online mode, while NIAT markets on-campus study. Whether the notice applies to a given campus is something only the notice, the university and the UGC can settle.

  • Careers360
Before you pay

Read the notice on the UGC website. Then ask the university in writing whether your programme is approved as regular, on-campus study and ask for the approval reference.

Claims

Placement and stipend figures mix different programmes

NxtWave's published outcomes carry a disclaimer that they come from several programmes, including online upskilling cohorts. Careers360 points out that the four-year B.Tech started in 2023 and that placements happen in the final year, so those figures cannot describe B.Tech graduates yet. The sources disagree on when the first batch finishes: Careers360 says 2027, while a review site says 2028 to 2029.

The ₹2.87 crore internship stipend total is shown without the number of students or the amount each received. The ₹7 LPA average often quoted is described by one review site as covering all NxtWave programmes, not NIAT alone.

  • Careers360
  • New Age Colleges
Before you pay

Ask for placement data for the B.Tech programme only, by batch, with the number of students placed, the median salary and the number who took part. If no batch has graduated yet, ask them to say so.

Money

Sales pressure, EMIs and hard-to-get refunds

A college-review site lists high-pressure enrolment tactics, concern about being locked into long loan commitments, and a narrow refund window as cons. A student-facing fee page says NIAT does not offer fee reimbursement, only performance-based waivers.

Two complaints on the Voxya consumer forum, from June and October 2025, describe students who could not keep paying EMIs and were told cancellation was not possible after the enrolment window. Both concern NxtWave's other programmes, not the B.Tech, so they show how refund requests have been handled, not what happens at NIAT.

  • New Age Colleges
  • CollegeDekho
  • Voxya, Jun 2025
  • Voxya, Oct 2025
Before you pay

Get the refund and cancellation terms in writing before any payment. Read the loan agreement yourself, note who the lender is, and do not sign because of a same-day deadline. If a dispute does arise, you can file a complaint through the National Consumer Helpline (1915).

Trust

People who recommend NIAT can be paid for it

NIAT runs a referral page that offers ₹40,000 each time a student someone recommends gets admission, and it invites second-year intermediate students to refer others. New Age Colleges also reports that positive online reviews have been manufactured or incentivised, though it gives no examples.

That does not mean any given recommendation is dishonest. It means a glowing post, video or school-corridor tip may come from someone with a financial reason to give it.

  • NIAT referral page
  • New Age Colleges
Before you pay

Ask any recommender whether they earn from referrals. Speak to current students you found yourself, not ones the counsellor introduced.

Campus

Facilities that differ from what was promised

Student reviews on CollegeDekho and Shiksha describe gaps between the pitch and the campus. A Mangalore student says the campus did not provide everything promised at admission and has few events, and compares it unfavourably with the Hyderabad campus. A Hyderabad review says hostels were first provided but are no longer available, so students live in PGs or flats. Another Hyderabad review says promised indoor games and gaming courts do not exist and the classroom air conditioning often needs repair. A Jaipur student reports poor hostel food and very weak mobile signal.

Other reviews of the same campuses are positive, so conditions appear to vary by campus and by year.

  • CollegeDekho, Mangalore
  • CollegeDekho, Hyderabad
  • CollegeDekho, Jaipur
  • Shiksha, Hyderabad
Before you pay

Visit the exact campus you would attend. Get hostel, transport and sports facilities listed in the admission paperwork, and ask who runs the hostel and whose rules apply.

Teaching

Little published about who actually teaches

New Age Colleges says the official site names mentors but gives no detail on faculty or on what mentorship involves. A CollegeDekho review for the Bangalore campus says feedback on faculty is mixed and that some critics say certain trainers are fresh graduates. A Shiksha reviewer for Hyderabad praised placements and infrastructure but said faculty support was poor, while another reviewer from the same campus was satisfied with faculty and curriculum.

  • New Age Colleges
  • CollegeDekho, Bangalore
  • Shiksha, Hyderabad
Before you pay

Ask for the faculty list for your campus with qualifications and experience. Ask which courses the university teaches for degree credit and which are NIAT's own training.

02 / THE OTHER SIDE

What NIAT says.

A fair assessment separates the official position from independent student accounts.

The official position

  • NIAT says it is an upskilling programme, not a university, and that the B.Tech is awarded by UGC-recognised, AICTE-approved partner universities.
  • It says placement is not guaranteed and depends on the learner's skills and performance.
  • Its training is described as non-credit and not affecting degree progression.

What many students say

  • Several reviewers praise learning to code from the first semester, projects and a modern campus.
  • Some report paid internships in their first or second year, though others say outcomes depend on the individual.
  • One explainer blog concludes NIAT is not a scam and not a guaranteed route to a job. That blog also promotes an enrolment code, so weigh it accordingly.
